Policies

Shopping with Moda Store

Everything you need to know about delivery, returns, refunds, payments, and how we protect your information when you shop with us.

Shipping & Delivery

  • Orders are prepared for shipping within 1–2 business days.
  • Delivery times may vary depending on your location in Egypt and the selected delivery method.
  • Once your order is shipped, you will receive the available tracking information so you can follow your delivery.
  • Delivery fees are calculated at checkout based on your delivery location and the available shipping option.
  • Please make sure your phone number, address, and delivery details are correct before confirming your order.

Returns & Exchanges

  • We accept eligible return requests within 14 days of receiving your order.
  • Returned items must be unworn, unused, and in their original condition with the original packaging and tags when applicable.
  • To request a return or exchange, contact our support team with your order details and the reason for the request.
  • Exchanges depend on the availability of the requested size or item.
  • Items that have been used, damaged, or returned without their original condition may not be eligible for return.

Refunds

  • Once an approved return is received and inspected, we will process the applicable refund.
  • Refunds for online payments are returned through the original payment method whenever supported by the payment provider.
  • For cash-on-delivery orders, the refund process may require additional information from the customer.
  • The time required for a refund to appear may vary depending on the payment provider or bank.
  • If you receive an incorrect or faulty item, please contact us as soon as possible so we can help resolve the issue.

Privacy

  • We collect the information needed to create and manage your account, process orders, and provide customer support.
  • Your contact and delivery information is used to fulfill your orders and communicate important order updates.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
  • We take reasonable measures to protect the information associated with your account and orders.
  • If you have questions about your personal information, please contact our support team.
